Commercial flexibility

Two paths to
cleaner power.

Whether your priority is ownership or operating flexibility, we help structure a solution that fits your balance sheet and energy goals.

CAPEX

Your solar asset.

Own your system and benefit directly from the energy it generates.

  • Upfront investment
  • Long-term asset ownership
  • Direct electricity savings
OPEX

Pay as you go.

An investor funds and owns the plant while you pay for the electricity consumed, typically below the DISCOM tariff.

  • Zero upfront investment
  • Monthly payment for electricity used
  • Plant transfer after the PPA period
