Execution Pipeline

From initial feasibility to lifelong generation.

Our systematic EPC lifecycle ensures streamlined statutory approvals, quality installations, and reliable power generation.

STAGE 01

Site Feasibility

Satellite survey, structural load assessment, shadow modeling, and electricity bill OCR to evaluate optimal capacity.

STAGE 02

Engineering & Approvals

Custom electrical schematics, civil structural designs, MSEDCL/DISCOM net-metering applications, and statutory approvals.

STAGE 03

Procurement & EPC

Supply of Tier-1 solar modules, inverters, robust mounting structures, followed by precision installation and commissioning.

STAGE 04

Monitoring & O&M

Continuous generation tracking, preventive maintenance cycles, and dedicated on-ground support for maximum plant uptime.
